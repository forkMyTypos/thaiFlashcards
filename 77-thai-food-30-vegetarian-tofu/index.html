Thai Food – Vegetarian & Tofu: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for thai food: vegetarian & tofu in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with เต้าหู้ (tofu), เต้าหู้ทอด (fried tofu), เต้าหู้ผัด (stir-fried tofu), เต้าหู้ทรงเครื่อง (tofu with toppings and sauce). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
