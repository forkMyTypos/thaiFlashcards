Thai Food – Regional sauces and dips: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: regional sauces and dips in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with น้ำพริก (chili dip), น้ำพริกกะปิ (shrimp-paste chili dip), น้ำพริกหนุ่ม (green chili dip), น้ำพริกอ่อง (tomato and pork chili dip). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
