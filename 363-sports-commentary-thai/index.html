Sports Commentary Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for sports commentary thai in the Sports In Thai part of the course, starting with จังหวะ (rhythm; beat; timing), โอกาส (opportunity), โอกาสทอง (golden opportunity), พุ่ง (rush | shoot forward | surge). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
