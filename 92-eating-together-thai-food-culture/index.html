Eating Together & Thai Food Culture: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for eating together & thai food culture in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with กินด้วยกัน (eat together), กินข้าวด้วยกัน (eat together), โต๊ะอาหาร (dining table), กับข้าว (dishes eaten with rice). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
