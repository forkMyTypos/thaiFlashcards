Indirectness & Reading the Room in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for indirectness & reading the room in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with พูดอ้อมๆ (speak indirectly), พูดตรงๆ (speak frankly), ไม่กล้าพูด (don't dare to say it), ไม่กล้าขอ (don't dare to ask). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
