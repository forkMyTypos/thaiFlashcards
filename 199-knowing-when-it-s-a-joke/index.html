Knowing When It's a Joke in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for knowing when it's a joke in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with พูดเล่น (say it as a joke), ล้อเล่น (joke / kid around), แซวเล่น (tease jokingly), ขำๆ (just for fun / joking). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
