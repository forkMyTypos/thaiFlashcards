Inviting Someone Somewhere in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for inviting someone somewhere in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with ไปด้วยกันไหม (shall we go together?), ไปไหม (shall we go?), ไปกินข้าวกันไหม (want to go eat together?), ไปดูหนังกันไหม (want to see a movie?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
