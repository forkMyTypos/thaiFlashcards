Reading Between the Lines in Thai: 19 words with audio

This lesson teaches 19 Thai words for reading between the lines in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with พูดอ้อมๆ (speak indirectly), พูดตรงๆ (speak frankly), ไม่ได้พูดตรงๆ (didn't say it directly), พูดเป็นนัยๆ (speak implicitly / hint at something). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
