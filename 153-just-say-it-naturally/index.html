Just Say It Naturally in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for just say it naturally in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with พูดง่ายๆ (simply put), พูดตรงๆ (speak frankly), เอาจริงๆ (honestly / seriously), จริงๆ แล้ว (actually). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
