Thai Food – Drinks: 22 words with audio

Order common Thai drinks and describe them as hot, cold, sweet, or fresh

น้ำ
náam
high
Noun
water, liquid, fluid, juice, river
Remember it by
nah

A person casually waves away a glass of water while saying, “Nah.”

In a sentence
  • ช่วยเอาน้ำให้ฉันหน่อย
    chûai ao náam hâi chǎn nɔ̀i
    Please get me some water.
  • ใส่น้ำตาลนิดเดียวก็พอ
    sài náam-dtaan nít diao gɔ̂ɔ phɔɔ
    Just a little sugar is enough.
  • น้ำจิ้มนี้อร่อยมาก
    náam-jîm níi à-rɔ̀i mâak
    This dipping sauce is very delicious.
