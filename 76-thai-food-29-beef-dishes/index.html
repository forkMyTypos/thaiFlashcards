In a sentence

    nʉ́ʉa gàp sà-làt khráp
    Meat and salad, please.\
  • เนื้อหรือกุ้งครับ
    nʉ́ʉa rʉ̌ʉ gûng khráp
    Meat or shrimp?\

    nʉ́ʉa phrɔ́ɔm láew khráp
    The meat is ready.\

Thai Food – Beef Dishes: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: beef dishes in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with เนื้อ (meat), เนื้อย่าง (grilled beef), เนื้อทอด (fried beef), เนื้อแดดเดียว (sun-dried beef). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
