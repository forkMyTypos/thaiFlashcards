Recognise numbers when Thai speakers say them naturally and quickly

หนึ่ง
nùeng
low
Adjective; Numeral
one, same, single
Remember it by

A nun holding up exactly one finger.

In a sentence
  • หนึ่งล้านบาท
    nùeng láan bàat
    One million baht.
  • หนึ่งพันบาทค่ะ
    nùeng phan bàat khâ
    One thousand baht.
  • หนึ่งหรือสอง
    nùeng rǔe sɔ̌ɔng
    One or two.

Thai Numbers in Real Speech: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for thai numbers in real speech in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with หนึ่ง (one), สอง (two), สาม (three), สี่ (four). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
