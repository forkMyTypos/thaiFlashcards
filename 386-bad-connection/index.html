Bad Connection in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for bad connection in the Thai Phone Calls part of the course, starting with เสียงไม่ชัด (the sound isn't clear), ได้ยินไหม (can you hear me?), ไม่ได้ยิน (cannot hear), เสียงเบา (soft / quiet voice). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
