Hotels & Accommodation in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for hotels & accommodation in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with เช็กอิน (check in), เช็กเอาต์ (check out), ห้องพัก (room), คีย์การ์ด (key card). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
