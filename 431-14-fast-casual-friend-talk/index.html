14. Fast & Casual Friend Talk in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 14. fast & casual friend talk in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with เออ (yeah / uh-huh), เออใช่ (yeah), เอาน่า (come on / don't worry), เออหน่า (come on / it's okay). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
