Taxis, Ride Apps & Getting Picked Up in Thai: 12 words…

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for taxis, ride apps & getting picked up in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with เรียกรถ (call a car / taxi), เรียกแท็กซี่ (call a taxi), คนขับ (driver), มารับ (come pick up). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
