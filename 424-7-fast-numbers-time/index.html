7. Fast Numbers & Time in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 7. fast numbers & time in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with กี่โมง (what time?), โมงหนึ่ง (one o'clock), สองโมง (two o'clock), เที่ยง (noon | midday). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
