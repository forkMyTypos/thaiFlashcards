10. When You Lose the Thread in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 10. when you lose the thread in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with คุยกันถึงไหนแล้ว (where were we in the conversation?), ผมตามไม่ทันแล้ว (I've lost track), สรุปสั้นๆ ให้หน่อย (give me a quick summary), ตกลงเรื่องอะไรกันแน่ (what exactly are we discussing?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
