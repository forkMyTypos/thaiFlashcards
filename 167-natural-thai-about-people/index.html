Natural Thai About People: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural thai about people in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with คนนี้ (this person), คนนั้น (that person), เขาเอง (him/her personally), เขาเป็นคน... (he/she is the kind of person who...). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
