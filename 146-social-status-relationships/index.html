Social Status & Relationships in Thai: 19 words with audio

This lesson teaches 19 Thai words for social status & relationships in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ให้เกียรติ (show respect / give honor), ให้ความเคารพ (show respect), เคารพผู้ใหญ่ (respect elders), เกรงใจ (be considerate | feel reluctant to impose). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
