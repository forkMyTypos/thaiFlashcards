Social Conflict & Difficult Conversations in Thai: 21…

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for social conflict & difficult conversations in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with มีปัญหากัน (have a problem with each other), ทะเลาะกัน (argue with each other), ไม่พอใจ (dissatisfied / unhappy), ไม่พอใจที่ (unhappy about). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
