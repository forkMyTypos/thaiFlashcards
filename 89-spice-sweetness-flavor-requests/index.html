Spice, Sweetness & Flavor Requests in Thai: 26 words with…

This lesson teaches 26 Thai words for spice, sweetness & flavor requests in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with เผ็ด (spicy), เผ็ดมาก (very spicy), เผ็ดน้อย (mildly spicy), ไม่เผ็ด (not spicy). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
