Banter Between Close Friends in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for banter between close friends in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with มึง (you; informal/rough pronoun), กู (I; me), กูบอกแล้ว (I told you), มึงเห็นไหม (did you see?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

Vocabulary (15 words)

มึง mueng
you; informal/rough pronoun
กู guu
I; me (very informal)
กูบอกแล้ว guu bòk láew
I told you
มึงเห็นไหม mueng hĕn mái
did you see?
มึงนี่นะ mueng nîi ná
you, seriously...
กูว่าแล้ว guu wâa láew
I knew it
มึงนี่มัน... mueng nîi man...
you really are...
เออ กูรู้ er guu rúu
yeah, I know
กูไม่ไหวแล้ว guu mâi wăi láew
I can't take it anymore
มึงทำอะไรเนี่ย mueng tham à-rai nîia
what the hell are you doing?
ไอ้นี่ âi nîi
this guy / you idiot
ไอ้นั่น âi nân
that guy / that idiot
เออ มึงก็ว่าไป er mueng gôr wâa bpai
yeah, whatever you say
มึงเอ๊ย mueng oei
oh, you...
กูละเบื่อมึง guu lá bèua mueng
I'm so sick of you
