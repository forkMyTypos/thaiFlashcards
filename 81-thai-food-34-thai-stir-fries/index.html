Thai Food – Thai Stir-Fries: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for thai food: thai stir-fries in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ผัดกะเพรา (stir-fried meat with holy basil), ผัดไทย (pad thai), ผัดซีอิ๊ว (stir-fried noodles with soy sauce), ราดหน้า (noodles with gravy). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
