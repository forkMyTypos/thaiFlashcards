Persuasion & Negotiation in Thai: 19 words with audio

This lesson teaches 19 Thai words for persuasion & negotiation in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ผมอยากเสนอว่า (I'd like to suggest that), ฉันอยากเสนอว่า (I'd like to suggest that), ถ้าเรา...ได้ก็น่าจะดี (it would be good if we could...), น่าจะเป็นทางออกที่ดี (that should be a good solution). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
