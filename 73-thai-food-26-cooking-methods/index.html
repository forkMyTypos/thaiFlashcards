This lesson teaches 43 Thai words for thai food: cooking methods in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ต้ม (boil), นึ่ง (steamed), ทอด (to fry), ผัด (stir-fry). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
