Asking Simple Questions in Thai: 21 words with audio

Basic Thai conversations for asking about people, things, places, and everyday situations

อะไร
à-rai
low-mid
Pronoun
what?, something, anything
Remember it by
A rye (bread)

A rye (bread). What bread is this ?

In a sentence
  • ฉันไม่รู้ว่าจะกินอะไรดี
    chǎn mâi rúu wâa jà gin à-rai dii
    I don't know what I should eat.
  • คุณอยากกินอะไรครับ
    khun yàak gin à-rai khráp
    What would you like to eat?
  • คุณชอบดื่มอะไรคะ
    khun chɔ̂ɔp dùuem à-rai khá
    What do you like to drink?

Asking Simple Questions in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for asking simple questions in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with อะไร (what?), ใคร (who?), ที่ไหน (where), เมื่อไหร่ (when). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
