Social Taboos & Sensitive Topics in Thai: 15 words with…

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for social taboos & sensitive topics in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with เรื่องส่วนตัว (personal matter), เรื่องละเอียดอ่อน (sensitive matter), เรื่องต้องห้าม (taboo / forbidden topic), ไม่ควรถาม (shouldn't ask). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
