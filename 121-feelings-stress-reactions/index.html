Feelings, Stress & Reactions in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for feelings, stress & reactions in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with รู้สึกยังไง (how do you feel?), รู้สึกดี (feel good), รู้สึกไม่ดี (feel bad), รู้สึกเหนื่อย (feel tired). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
