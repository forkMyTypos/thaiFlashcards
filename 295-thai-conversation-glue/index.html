In a sentence
  • ถ้าคุณชอบชาก็เอาชาครับ
    thâa khun chɔ̂ɔp chaa gâw ao chaa khráp
    If you like tea, then have tea.\
  • ถ้ากาแฟร้อนก็ขอชาเย็นค่ะ
    thâa gaa-fae rɔ́ɔn gâw khɔ̌ɔ chaa yen khâ
    If the coffee is hot, I'll have cold tea.\

    khun mâi ao khâao phǒm gâw mâi ao khráp
    If you don't want rice, I won't have it either.\

Thai Conversation Glue: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for thai conversation glue in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with ก็ (then), แล้วก็ (and then), แต่ว่า (but; however), เพราะว่า (because). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
