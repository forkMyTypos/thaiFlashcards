Sarcasm & “Yeah, Right” in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for sarcasm & “yeah, right” in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with เหรอ (really?), จริงเหรอ (really?), จริงดิ (really?), เอาจริงดิ (seriously?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
