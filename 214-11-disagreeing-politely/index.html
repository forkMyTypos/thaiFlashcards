11. Disagreeing Politely in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 11. disagreeing politely in the Professional Thai part of the course, starting with ขออนุญาตแสดงความคิดเห็น (may I give my opinion), ผมขอเสนออีกมุมหนึ่ง (I'd like to offer another perspective), อาจจะ (might), อาจมีความเป็นไปได้ว่า (it may be possible that). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
