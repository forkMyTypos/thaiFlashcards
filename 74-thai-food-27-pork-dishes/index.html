Thai Food – Pork Dishes: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: pork dishes in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with หมูกรอบ (crispy pork), หมูแดง (red pork), หมูหวาน (sweet pork), หมูทอด (fried pork). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
