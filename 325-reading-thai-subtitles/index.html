Reading Thai Subtitles: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for reading thai subtitles in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ซับ (subtitles), คำบรรยาย (subtitles / captions), แปล (translate), อ่าน (to read). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
