11. Group Discussion and Problem Solving in Thai: 10 words…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 11. group discussion and problem solving in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with ปัญหาหลักคืออะไร (what's the main problem?), เรามีทางเลือกอะไรบ้าง (what options do we have?), ข้อดีข้อเสียมีอะไรบ้าง (what are the pros and cons?), ใครรับผิดชอบส่วนไหน (who's responsible for which part?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
