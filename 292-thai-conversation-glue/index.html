Thai Conversation Glue: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for thai conversation glue in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with ก็ (then), แล้วก็ (and then), คือ (to be), แบบ (style). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
