Checking In in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for checking in in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with เคาน์เตอร์ (counter / front desk), แผนกต้อนรับ (reception), บัตรประชาชน (national ID card), หนังสือเดินทาง (passport). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
