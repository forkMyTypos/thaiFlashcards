Adverb; Conjunction; Particle

loud

Time and sentence building in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for time and sentence building in the Support Words part of the course, starting with แล้ว (then), ก่อน (before), หลังจาก (after), ต้อง (must). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck. When you are ready, practise the same words in 60 example sentences.
