Adverb; Conjunction; Particle

Remember it by
loud
In a sentence
  • เราสั่งอาหารแล้ว
    rao sàng aa-hǎan láaeo
    We've already ordered the food.
  • ข้าวหมดแล้ว
    khâao mòt láaeo
    The rice is all gone.
  • กระทะร้อนแล้ว
    grà-thá rɔ́ɔn láaeo
    The pan is hot now.

Time and sentence building in Thai: 12 words with audio

Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.
