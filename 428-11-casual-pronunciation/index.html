อะไร
à-rai
low-mid
Pronoun
what?, something, anything
Remember it by
In a sentence
  • ฉันไม่รู้ว่าจะกินอะไรดี
    chǎn mâi rúu wâa jà gin à-rai dii
    I don't know what I should eat.
  • วันนี้ร้านนี้มีเมนูอะไรบ้าง
    wan-níi ráan níi mii mee-nuu à-rai bâang
    What dishes does this restaurant have today?
  • คุณอยากกินอะไร
    khun yàak gin à-rai
    What do you want to eat?

11. Casual Pronunciation in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 11. casual pronunciation in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with อะไร (what?), อะไรนะ (what was that?), อย่างไร (how?), ยังไง (how). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
