Critiquing Entertainment in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for critiquing entertainment in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with บท (script / role), บทดี (good script), บทอ่อน (weak script), ดำเนินเรื่องช้า (slow-paced). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
