What Are You Doing? in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for what are you doing? in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ทำอะไรอยู่ (what are you doing?), กำลังทำอะไร (what are you doing right now?), ทำงานอยู่ (I'm working), เรียนอยู่ (I'm studying). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
