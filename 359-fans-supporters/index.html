Fans & Supporters in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for fans & supporters in the Sports In Thai part of the course, starting with แฟนบอล (football fan), กองเชียร์ (supporters / cheering section), เชียร์ (cheer / support), เชียร์ทีม (support a team). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
