Education & Schools in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for education & schools in the News & Formal Thai part of the course, starting with การศึกษา (education), นักเรียน (student), นักศึกษา (student), ครู (teacher). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
