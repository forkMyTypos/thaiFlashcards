Reading the tone off the spelling

Thai does not write the tone down. It follows from four things: the class of the first consonant, whether the syllable is live or dead, the length of the vowel, and the tone mark. Learn this once and you can say a word you have never seen.

no mark, liveno mark, dead (short)no mark, dead (long)◌่◌้◌๊◌๋
midmidlowlowlowfallinghighrising
highrisinglowlowlowfalling——
lowmidhighfallingfallinghigh——

A low-class consonant cannot make a rising tone on its own, which is why ห is written before one to lend it a high class — หมา is mǎa, not maa. Across two syllables the same trick is อักษรนำ: ขณะ is khà-nà because ข hands its class to ณ.
