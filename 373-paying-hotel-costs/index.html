Paying & Hotel Costs in Thai: 13 words with audio

This lesson teaches 13 Thai words for paying & hotel costs in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with ราคา (price), ค่าห้อง (room charge), ค่าอาหาร (food charge), ค่าใช้จ่าย (expense; expenditure). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
