Cultural Nuance & Unspoken Meaning in Thai: 20 words with…

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for cultural nuance & unspoken meaning in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with เกรงใจมาก (feel very reluctant to impose), ไม่กล้าพูด (don't dare to say it), ไม่กล้าขอ (don't dare to ask), พูดตามตรง (honestly / frankly). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
