Booking, Appointments & Reservations in Thai: 12 words…

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for booking, appointments & reservations in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with จอง (book), การจอง (reservation), นัด (appointment; date; arrange to meet), ว่าง (empty; free; vacant). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
