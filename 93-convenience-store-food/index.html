Convenience Store Food in Thai: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for convenience store food in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ข้าวกล่อง (boxed meal), อาหารพร้อมกิน (ready-to-eat food), อาหารแช่แข็ง (frozen food), บะหมี่กึ่งสำเร็จรูป (instant noodles). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
