Arguing a Point Naturally in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for arguing a point naturally in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ผมไม่ค่อยเห็นด้วย (I don't really agree), ฉันไม่ค่อยเห็นด้วย (I don't really agree), ผมเห็นต่าง (I see it differently), ฉันเห็นต่าง (I see it differently). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
