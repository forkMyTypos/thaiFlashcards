Smoothies, Shakes & Fresh Drinks in Thai: 29 words with…

Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
