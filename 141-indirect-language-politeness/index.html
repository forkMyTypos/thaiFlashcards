Indirect Language & Politeness in Thai: 19 words with audio

This lesson teaches 19 Thai words for indirect language & politeness in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ไม่ทราบว่า (may I ask / I wonder if), พอจะ...ได้ไหม (would it be possible to...?), จะเป็นไปได้ไหม (would it be possible?), รบกวนหน่อยได้ไหม (could I trouble you for a moment?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
