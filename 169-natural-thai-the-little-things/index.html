Natural Thai – The Little Things: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural thai: the little things in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with ก็ว่าอยู่ (that's what I thought / I was wondering), ว่าแล้ว (I knew it / called it), นึกว่า... (I thought that...), นึกว่าเป็น... (I thought it was...). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
