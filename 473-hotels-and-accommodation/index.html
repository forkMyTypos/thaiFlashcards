In a sentence

    jàak sǎm-nák-ngaan thǔeng roong-raem khráp
    From the office to the hotel.

    sǎm-nák-ngaan yùu rá-wàang roong-raem gàp wát khráp
    The office is between the hotel and the temple.

    roong-raem mii sǎm-nák-ngaan mái khráp
    Does the hotel have an office?

ⓘ About this lesson

Hotels and accommodation in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for hotels and accommodation in the Crash Course part of the course, starting with โรงแรม (hotel), ห้อง (room), ห้องพัก (room), จอง (book). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
