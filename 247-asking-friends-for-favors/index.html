Asking Friends for Favors in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for asking friends for favors in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with ช่วยหน่อยได้ไหม (could you help me?), ช่วยหน่อย (please help), ขอถามหน่อย (can I ask something?), ขอให้ช่วยหน่อย (could you help me?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
