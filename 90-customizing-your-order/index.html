  • ฉันขอเพิ่มข้าวได้ไหม
    chǎn khɔ̌ɔ phə̂əm khâao dâai mái
    Can I get more rice?

    khɔ̌ɔ khâao phə̂əm ìik nʉ̀ng jaan

    sài grà-thiam phə̂əm ìik nɔ̀i
    Add a little more garlic.

Customizing Your Order in Thai: 25 words with audio

This lesson teaches 25 Thai words for customizing your order in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with เพิ่ม (to add), ลด (to reduce), เพิ่มข้าว (add more rice), เพิ่มผัก (add more vegetables). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
