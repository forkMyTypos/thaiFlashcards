Talking About Lyrics in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for talking about lyrics in the Thai Music & Lyrics part of the course, starting with ความหมาย (meaning), แปลว่า (means / translates as), หมายถึง (mean), เนื้อหาของเพลง (content of the song). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
