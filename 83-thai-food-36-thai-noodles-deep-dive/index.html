Thai Food – Thai Noodles Deep Dive: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for thai food: thai noodles deep dive in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ก๋วยเตี๋ยว (noodles), ก๋วยเตี๋ยวน้ำ (noodle soup with broth), ก๋วยเตี๋ยวแห้ง (dry noodles), ก๋วยเตี๋ยวหมู (pork noodle soup). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
