ⓘ About this lesson

Phone and internet in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for phone and internet in the Crash Course part of the course, starting with โทรศัพท์ (telephone), มือถือ (mobile phone), อินเทอร์เน็ต (internet), ไวไฟ (Wi-Fi). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
