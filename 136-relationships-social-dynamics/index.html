Relationships & Social Dynamics in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for relationships & social dynamics in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with สนิทกัน (be close with each other), ไม่ค่อยสนิท (not very close), เข้ากันได้ (get along), เข้ากันไม่ได้ (don't get along). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
