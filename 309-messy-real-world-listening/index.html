Messy Real-World Listening in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for messy real-world listening in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with แป๊บนะ (wait a sec), เดี๋ยวนะ (wait a second), อะไรนะเนี่ย (what is this? / what did you say?), ฟังไม่ทันเลย (I really couldn't catch that). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
