5. Group Jokes and Banter in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 5. group jokes and banter in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with มุกนี้เคยได้ยินแล้ว (I've heard this joke before), ใครเริ่มก่อนเนี่ย (who started this?), อย่าแกล้งกันสิ (stop messing with each other), พวกแกนี่นะ (you lot). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
