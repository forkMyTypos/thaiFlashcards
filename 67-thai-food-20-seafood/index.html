Thai Food – Seafood: 25 words with audio

This lesson teaches 25 Thai words for thai food: seafood in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with กุ้ง (shrimp), ปู (crab), ปลาหมึก (squid), หอย (shellfish). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
