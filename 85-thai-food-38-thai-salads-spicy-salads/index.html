Thai Food – Thai Salads & Spicy Salads: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for thai food: thai salads & spicy salads in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ยำ (spicy mixed salad), ยำวุ้นเส้น (glass noodle salad), ยำมาม่า (instant noodle salad), ยำหมูยอ (Vietnamese-style pork sausage salad). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
