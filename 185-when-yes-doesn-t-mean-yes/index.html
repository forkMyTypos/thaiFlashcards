When “Yes” Doesn't Mean Yes in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for when “yes” doesn't mean yes in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with ได้ (to get), ได้ครับ (yes / okay), ได้ค่ะ (yes / okay), ก็ได้ (that's fine / okay). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
