Listening Basics in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for listening basics in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with ฟัง (to listen), ฟังออก (understand by listening), ฟังไม่ออก (can't understand what I hear), ได้ยิน (hear). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
