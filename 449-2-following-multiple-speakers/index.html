2. Following Multiple Speakers in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 2. following multiple speakers in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with เมื่อกี้ใครพูดนะ (who said that just now?), เขาหมายถึงใคร (who does he or she mean?), คนไหนนะ (which person again?), คนที่นั่งตรงนั้น (the person sitting over there). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
