Getting Around as a Tourist in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for getting around as a tourist in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with ไปไหน (where are you going?), ไปยังไง (how do I get there?), อยู่ที่ไหน (where do you live/stay?), ใกล้ไหม (is it near?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
