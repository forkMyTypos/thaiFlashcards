Making Plans Under Pressure in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for making plans under pressure in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ไม่มีเวลาแล้ว (there's no time left), ต้องรีบแล้ว (we have to hurry now), เอายังไงดี (what should we do?), เอาแบบไหนดี (which way should we do it?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
