ⓘ About this lesson

Numbers and counting in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for numbers and counting in the Crash Course part of the course, starting with ศูนย์ (center), หนึ่ง (one), สอง (two), สาม (three). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
