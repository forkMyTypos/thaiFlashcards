Real-life Thai: 14 words with audio

This lesson teaches 14 Thai words for real-life thai in the Beginner part of the course, starting with จริงๆ แล้ว (actually), แต่ว่า (but; however), เพราะว่า (because), ถ้าอย่างนั้น (if that's the case). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
