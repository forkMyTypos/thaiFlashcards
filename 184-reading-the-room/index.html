Reading the Room in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for reading the room in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with ดูบรรยากาศ (read the atmosphere), บรรยากาศ (atmosphere), ดูอารมณ์ (gauge someone's mood), จับอารมณ์ (pick up on someone's mood). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

read the atmosphere
บรรยากาศ ban-yaa-gàat
atmosphere, ambiance, environment
