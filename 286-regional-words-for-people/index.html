Regional Words for People in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for regional words for people in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with อ้าย (older male / older brother), ปี้ (older sibling / older person), เอื้อย (older sister / older female), อุ้ย (grandparent / elderly person). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
