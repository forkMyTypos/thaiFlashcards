Real Thai Entertainment Chat: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for real thai entertainment chat in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ดูเรื่องนี้หรือยัง (have you watched this yet?), เรื่องนี้สนุกมาก (this show is really good), ตอนล่าสุดเป็นไง (how was the latest episode?), ฉันติดเรื่องนี้มาก (I'm really hooked on this show). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
