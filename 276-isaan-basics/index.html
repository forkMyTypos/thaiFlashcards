Isaan Basics in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for isaan basics in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with อีสาน (Northeast), บ้านเฮา (our home / our place), เฮา (we / us / I), เจ้า (you). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
