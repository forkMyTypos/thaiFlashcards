Real-World Advanced Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for real-world advanced thai in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ขอพูดตรงๆ ก่อน (let me be honest first), ผมไม่ได้จะว่าอะไรนะ (I don't mean anything by it), ฉันไม่ได้จะว่าอะไรนะ (I don't mean anything by it), แต่ผมรู้สึกว่า (but I feel that). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
