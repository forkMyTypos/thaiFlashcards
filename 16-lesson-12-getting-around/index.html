Getting around in Thai: 14 words with audio

Learn 14 Thai words for getting around, including รถ (car), คัน (stick), สนามบิน (airport). With audio, romanization, free flashcards and a quiz.

รถ
rót
high
Classifier; Noun
car, vehicle, [classifier for vehicles]
Remember it by
SentencesView sentence lesson →
รถจากสนามบิน
rót jàak sà-nǎam-bin
A car from the airport.
รถคันนี้
rót khan níi
This car.
♂male
มีรถจากสนามบินครับ
mii rót jàak sà-nǎam-bin khráp
There’s a car from the airport.
♂male

Is there a car from the hotel?
♂male

A car for the airport.
♂male
