Explaining What Happened in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for explaining what happened in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with มีปัญหา (there was a problem), เกิดปัญหา (a problem occurred), รถเสีย (the vehicle broke down), รถติด (traffic jam). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
