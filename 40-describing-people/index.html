Describing people in Thai: 14 words with audio

This lesson teaches 14 Thai words for describing people in the Beginner part of the course, starting with หน้าตา (appearance; looks; face), ผมยาว (long hair), ผมสั้น (short hair), สูง (high). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
