Regional Words for Food & Eating in Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for regional words for food & eating in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with กิ๋น (eat), แซ่บ (delicious / tasty), หรอย (delicious / tasty), ข้าวเหนียว (sticky rice). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
