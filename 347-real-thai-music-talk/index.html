Real Thai Music Talk: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for real thai music talk in the Thai Music & Lyrics part of the course, starting with ช่วงนี้ฟังเพลงอะไร (what music have you been listening to lately?), เพลงนี้เพราะดี (this song is pretty good), เพลงนี้ติดหูมาก (this song is really catchy), ฟังแล้วอินมาก (really get into it when listening). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
