Thai Food – Breakfast and light meals: 16 words with audio

This lesson teaches 16 Thai words for thai food: breakfast and light meals in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with โจ๊ก (congee), โจ๊กหมู (pork congee), ข้าวต้ม (rice soup), ข้าวต้มปลา (fish rice soup). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
