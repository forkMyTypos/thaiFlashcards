Ask simple questions and understand the basic words used in replies.\. 20 Thai words with audio, including อะไร (what?), ใคร (who?), เมื่อไหร่ (when).

อะไร
à-rai
low-mid
Pronoun
what?, something, anything
Remember it by
A rye (bread)

A rye (bread). What bread is this ?

In a sentence
  • ฉันไม่รู้ว่าจะกินอะไรดี
    chǎn mâi rúu wâa jà gin à-rai dii
    I don't know what I should eat.
  • คุณอยากกินอะไรครับ
    khun yàak gin à-rai khráp
    What would you like to eat?
  • คุณชอบดื่มอะไรคะ
    khun chɔ̂ɔp dùuem à-rai khá
    What do you like to drink?

ⓘ About this lesson

Asking questions in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for asking questions in the Crash Course part of the course, starting with อะไร (what?), ใคร (who?), เมื่อไหร่ (when), ทำไม (why). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
