Switching Between Central & Regional Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for switching between central & regional thai in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with ภาษาไทยกลาง (Central Thai), ภาษาถิ่น (regional language / dialect), สำเนียง (accent), คำศัพท์ (vocabulary). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
