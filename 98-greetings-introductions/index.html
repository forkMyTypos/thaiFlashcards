Greetings & Introductions in Thai: 18 words with audio

This lesson teaches 18 Thai words for greetings & introductions in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with สวัสดี (hello), สวัสดีครับ (hello), สวัสดีค่ะ (hello), เป็นไงบ้าง (how are you?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
