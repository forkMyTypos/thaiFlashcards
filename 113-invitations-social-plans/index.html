Invitations & Social Plans in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for invitations & social plans in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ชวน (invite), ชวนไปไหน (invite someone to go somewhere), ชวนไปกินข้าว (invite someone to eat), ชวนไปเที่ยว (invite someone to go out). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
