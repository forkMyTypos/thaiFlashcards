People and family in Thai: 15 words with audio

Learn 15 Thai words for people and family, including เขา (he), เรา (we), คน (person). With audio, romanization, free flashcards and a quiz.

เขา

rising
Noun; Pronoun
he, she, they [can be impolite depending on context], mountain, horn
Remember it by

A man, and a woman point at each other with a cow in the background.
