Isaan Particles & Tone of Speech in Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for isaan particles & tone of speech in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with เด้อ (friendly softening particle), เด้อครับ (polite regional-style ending), เด้อค่ะ (polite regional-style ending), แหน่ (please / a little / softening particle). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
