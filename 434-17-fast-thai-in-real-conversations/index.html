17. Fast Thai in Real Conversations: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 17. fast thai in real conversations in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with พอดีว่า... (actually / the thing is...), คือ... (I mean...), เอาเป็นว่า... (let's say / let's settle on...), งั้นเอาเป็นว่า... (then let's say / let's go with...). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
