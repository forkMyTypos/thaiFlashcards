Calling Hotels & Businesses in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for calling hotels & businesses in the Thai Phone Calls part of the course, starting with เปิดไหมครับ (are you open?), เปิดถึงกี่โมงครับ (what time do you close?), ยังมีห้องว่างไหมครับ (do you still have rooms available?), ขอจองโต๊ะได้ไหมครับ (can I reserve a table?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
