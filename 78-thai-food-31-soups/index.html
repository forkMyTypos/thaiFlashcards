Thai Food – Soups: 30 words with audio

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for thai food: soups in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ต้มยำ (tom yum), ต้มยำกุ้ง (tom yum shrimp soup), ต้มยำไก่ (tom yum chicken soup), ต้มยำทะเล (tom yum seafood soup). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
