Natural Uncertainty in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural uncertainty in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with ไม่รู้เหมือนกัน (I don't know either), ไม่แน่ (maybe / not sure), ไม่แน่เหมือนกัน (I'm not sure either), น่าจะมั้ง (probably). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
