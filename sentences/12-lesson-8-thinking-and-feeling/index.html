This lesson has 44 Thai example sentences about thinking and feeling in the Beginner part of the course. Every sentence is written only with words you have met, and each one comes with romanization, tone colours and a natural English translation, and a recording you can slow down.

Read them one by one, switch to flashcards to test yourself, or take the quiz. Where the speaker's gender changes the wording (ผม or ฉัน, ครับ or ค่ะ), the other version is one tap away.
