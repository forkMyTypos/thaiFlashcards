rót yùu nâa bâan khráp
The car is in front of the house.
♂male

♂male

♂male

♀female

This lesson has 22 Thai example sentences about transport and personal items in the Beginner Core Vocabulary — Everyday nouns part of the course. Every sentence is written only with words you have met, and each one comes with romanization, tone colours and a natural English translation.

Read them one by one, switch to flashcards to test yourself, or take the quiz. Where the speaker's gender changes the wording (ผม or ฉัน, ครับ or ค่ะ), the other version is one tap away.
