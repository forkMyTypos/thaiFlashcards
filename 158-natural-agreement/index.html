Natural Agreement in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural agreement in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with ใช่ (yes), ใช่เลย (exactly), ใช่ๆ (yeah), เห็นด้วย (agree; concur). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
