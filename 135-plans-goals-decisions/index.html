Plans, Goals & Decisions in Thai: 22 words with audio

This lesson teaches 22 Thai words for plans, goals & decisions in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ตั้งใจจะ (intend to), ตั้งใจทำ (intend to do), วางแผนไว้ (have a plan), มีแผนจะ (plan to). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
