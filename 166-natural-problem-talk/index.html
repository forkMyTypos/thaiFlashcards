Natural Problem Talk in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural problem talk in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with มีปัญหา (there was a problem), เป็นอะไร (what's wrong?), เกิดอะไรขึ้น (what happened?), ทำไงดี (what should I do?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
