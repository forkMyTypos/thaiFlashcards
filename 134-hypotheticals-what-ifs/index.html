Hypotheticals & What-Ifs in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for hypotheticals & what-ifs in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ถ้า (if), ถ้าเป็นแบบนั้น (if that's the case), ถ้าอย่างนั้น (if that's the case), ถ้าเป็นผม (if I were you). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

if

if that's the case
ถ้าอย่างนั้น thâa-yàang-nán

if I were you

suppose it's like this
