Regional Thai in Real Conversations: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for regional thai in real conversations in the Regional Thai — Isaan / Northern / Southern part of the course, starting with บ่เป็นหยัง (it's okay / no problem), เป็นจั่งได๋ (how are you / how is it?), ไปไส (where are you going?), กิ๋นข้าวแล้วก่อ (have you eaten? — Northern). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
