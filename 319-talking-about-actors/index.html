Talking About Actors in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for talking about actors in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ดารา (star | celebrity | actor), นักร้อง (singer), พิธีกร (presenter / host), ผู้กำกับ (director). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
