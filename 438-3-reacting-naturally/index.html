3. Reacting Naturally in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 3. reacting naturally in the Thai Two Person Conversations part of the course, starting with โห (wow / damn), อ้าวเหรอ (oh), ไม่น่าเชื่อ (unbelievable), ดีจัง (that's great). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
