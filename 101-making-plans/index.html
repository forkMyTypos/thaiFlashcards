Making Plans in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for making plans in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ว่างไหม (are you free?), วันนี้ว่างไหม (are you free today?), พรุ่งนี้ว่างไหม (are you free tomorrow?), ไปไหม (shall we go?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

Vocabulary (21 words)

are you free?

are you free today?
พรุ่งนี้ว่างไหม phrûng-níi wâang mái
are you free tomorrow?
ไปไหม bpai-mǎi
shall we go?

ไปที่ไหนดี bpai thîi-nǎi dii
where should we go?
