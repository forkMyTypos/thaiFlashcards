15. Fast, Rude & Close-Friend Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 15. fast, rude & close-friend thai in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with กู (I; me), มึง (you; informal/rough pronoun), ไอ้นี่ (this guy / you idiot), ไอ้บ้า (you idiot / you crazy fool). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
