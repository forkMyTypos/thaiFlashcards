Restaurant Problems & Special Requests in Thai: 30 words…

This lesson teaches 30 Thai words for restaurant problems & special requests in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ยังไม่ได้ (not yet), ยังไม่มา (hasn't arrived yet), รอนาน (long wait), ช้า (slow). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
