When Friends Annoy You in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for when friends annoy you in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with รำคาญแล้วนะ (you're getting annoying now), พอเลย (enough already), หยุดเลย (stop it right now), อะไรนักหนา (what's the big deal?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

อะไรนักหนา à-rai nák-năa

พูดดีๆ phûut-dii-dii
watch how you talk / speak nicely
