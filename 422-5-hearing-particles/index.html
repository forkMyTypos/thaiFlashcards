5. Hearing Particles in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 5. hearing particles in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with ครับ (polite ending particle for male speakers), ค่ะ (polite particle used by females), คะ (polite question ending), นะ (particle used for softening or emphasizing). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
