Genres & Types in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for genres & types in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with หนังแอ็กชัน (action movie), หนังรัก (romance movie), หนังผี (horror movie), หนังตลก (comedy movie). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
