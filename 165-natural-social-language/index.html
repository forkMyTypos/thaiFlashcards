Natural Social Language in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural social language in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with สบายๆ (easy / relaxed), ตามสบายเลย (make yourself comfortable), ไม่ต้องเกรงใจ (don't feel obliged / don't be shy), เกรงใจจัง (I feel bad imposing). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

easy / relaxed
ตามสบายเลย dtaam sà-baai loei
make yourself comfortable
ไม่ต้องเกรงใจ mâi dtông greng-jai
don't feel obliged / don't be shy
เกรงใจจัง greng-jai jang
I feel bad imposing

thanks

sorry / excuse me

sorry about that
ไม่เป็นไรนะ mâi-bpen-rai ná
it's okay
โอเคนะ oh-kay ná
okay? / okay then

is that okay?
ได้หรือเปล่า dâi rĕu bplàao

whatever works for you
