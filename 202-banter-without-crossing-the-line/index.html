Banter Without Crossing the Line in Thai: 15 words with…

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for banter without crossing the line in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with ล้อเล่นได้ไหม (can I joke around?), พูดเล่นนะ (I'm joking), อย่าโกรธนะ (don't be mad), อย่าเพิ่งโกรธ (don't get angry yet). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

Vocabulary (15 words)

can I joke around?
พูดเล่นนะ phûut lên ná

don't be mad

I didn't mean to

just joking
