Negotiating & Finding Agreement in Thai: 24 words with audio

This lesson teaches 24 Thai words for negotiating & finding agreement in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ตกลงกัน (come to an agreement), ตกลงไหม (agree?), ตกลง (agree), เห็นด้วยไหม (do you agree?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
