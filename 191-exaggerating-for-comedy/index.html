Exaggerating for Comedy in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for exaggerating for comedy in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with อะไรจะขนาดนั้น (why so much / that much?), เว่อร์ไปแล้ว (that's way too exaggerated), เว่อร์เกิน (way too much), เกินไปแล้ว (that's too much). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
