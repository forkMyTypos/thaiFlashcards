Keeping a Conversation Going in Thai: 22 words with audio

This lesson teaches 22 Thai words for keeping a conversation going in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with แล้วต่อจากนั้นล่ะ (and what happened after that?), แล้วเป็นไงต่อ (and then what happened?), แล้วหลังจากนั้น (and after that), แล้วคุณคิดยังไง (and what do you think?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
