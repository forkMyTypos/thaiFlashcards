12. Natural Two-Person Conversation in Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 12. natural two-person conversation in the Thai Two Person Conversations part of the course, starting with เป็นไงบ้างช่วงนี้ (how have you been lately?), ก็เรื่อยๆ แล้วคุณล่ะ (same old), ช่วงนี้ยุ่งมากไหม (have you been very busy lately?), นิดหน่อย แต่ก็โอเค (a little). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
