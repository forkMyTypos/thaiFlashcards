Giving Reasons in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for giving reasons in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with เพราะ (because), เพราะว่า (because), เพราะอะไร (why), ก็เลย (so). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
