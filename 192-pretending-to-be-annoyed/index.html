Pretending to Be Annoyed in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for pretending to be annoyed in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with รำคาญแล้วนะ (you're getting annoying now), หมั่นไส้ (find someone irritating / be annoyed by someone), หมั่นไส้จริงๆ (you're really annoying), น่าหมั่นไส้ (annoyingly likeable / irritating). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
