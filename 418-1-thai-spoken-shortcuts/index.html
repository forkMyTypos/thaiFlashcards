1. Thai Spoken Shortcuts: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 1. thai spoken shortcuts in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with พูดว่า (say that / say), อะไร (what?), อะไรนะ (what was that?), ไม่เป็นไร (no problem). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
