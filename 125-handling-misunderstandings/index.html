Handling Misunderstandings in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for handling misunderstandings in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with หมายความว่าอะไร (what does that mean?), หมายถึงอะไร (what do you mean?), ผมหมายถึง (I mean), ฉันหมายถึง (I mean). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

what do you mean?
