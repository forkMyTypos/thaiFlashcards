Thai Food – Essential dishes: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: essential dishes in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ข้าว (rice), ข้าวสวย (steamed rice), ข้าวเหนียว (sticky rice), ข้าวผัด (fried rice). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
