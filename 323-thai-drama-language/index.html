Thai Drama Language: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for thai drama language in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ทำไมถึง (why did / why is it that), ฉันไม่เข้าใจ (I don't understand), ฉันขอโทษ (I'm sorry), อย่าทิ้งฉัน (don't leave me). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
