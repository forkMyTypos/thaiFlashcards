Entertainment Gossip in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for entertainment gossip in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ข่าวลือ (rumour), ข่าวบันเทิง (entertainment news), เรื่องจริง (true story / fact), จริงหรือเปล่า (is it true?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
