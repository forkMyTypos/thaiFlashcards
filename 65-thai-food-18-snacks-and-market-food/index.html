Thai Food – Snacks and market food: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: snacks and market food in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ลูกชิ้นปิ้ง (grilled meatballs), ไส้กรอก (sausage), หมูปิ้ง (grilled pork), ไก่ทอด (fried chicken). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
