Telling Short Stories in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for telling short stories in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with เมื่อวาน (yesterday), เมื่อเช้า (this morning), เมื่อกี้ (just now), เมื่อวันก่อน (the other day). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
