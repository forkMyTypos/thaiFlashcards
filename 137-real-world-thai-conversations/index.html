Real-World Thai Conversations: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for real-world thai conversations in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ขอถามอะไรหน่อย (can I ask you something?), มีเวลาไหม (do you have time?), มีเรื่องอยากคุย (there's something I want to talk about), คือผมว่า (well). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
