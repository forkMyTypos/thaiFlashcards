เกรงใจ: Social Consideration in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for เกรงใจ: social consideration in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with เกรงใจ (be considerate | feel reluctant to impose), ไม่ต้องเกรงใจ (don't feel obliged / don't be shy), เกรงใจจัง (I feel bad imposing), ไม่อยากรบกวน (I don't want to bother you). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
