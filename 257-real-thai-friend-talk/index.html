Real Thai Friend Talk: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for real thai friend talk in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with เออว่าไง (hey), มีไรวะ (what's up?), ไปไหนมาวะ (where have you been?), หายไปไหนมา (where have you disappeared to?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
