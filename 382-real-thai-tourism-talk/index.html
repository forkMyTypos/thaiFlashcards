Real Thai Tourism Talk: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for real thai tourism talk in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with ขอถามหน่อยครับ (may I ask something?), มีอะไรแนะนำไหมครับ (is there anything you recommend?), แถวนี้มีอะไรน่าเที่ยวบ้าง (what is worth visiting around here?), ถ้าไปที่นี่ต้องจองไหม (do I need to book to go here?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
