Getting Around in Thai: 23 words with audio

This lesson teaches 23 Thai words for getting around in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ไปไหน (where are you going?), ไปที่นี่ (go here), ไปตรงไหน (where do I go?), ไปทางไหน (which way?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
