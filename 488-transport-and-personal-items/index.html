ⓘ About this lesson

Transport and personal items in Thai: 8 words with audio

This lesson teaches 8 Thai words for transport and personal items in the Beginner Core Vocabulary — Everyday nouns part of the course, starting with รถ (car), รถยนต์ (car; automobile), รถไฟ (train; railway), รถเมล์ (bus). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck. When you are ready, practise the same words in 22 example sentences.
