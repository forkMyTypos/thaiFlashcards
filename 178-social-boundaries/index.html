Social Boundaries in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for social boundaries in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with ข้ามเส้น (cross the line), เกินขอบเขต (go beyond the boundary), ขอบเขต (boundary | scope | limit), ตั้งขอบเขต (set boundaries). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
