4. Agreeing and Disagreeing in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 4. agreeing and disagreeing in the Thai Two Person Conversations part of the course, starting with เห็นด้วย (agree; concur), เห็นด้วยเลย (totally agree), ก็จริงนะ (that's true), ไม่แน่ใจนะ (I'm not sure). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
