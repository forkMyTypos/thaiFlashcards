Talking About Your Trip in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for talking about your trip in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with เที่ยว (to travel), ท่องเที่ยว (to travel), นักท่องเที่ยว (tourist), เดินทาง (to travel). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
