Age, Status & Thai Address: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for age, status & thai address in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with พี่ (older sibling), น้อง (younger sibling), ลุง (uncle; older man), ป้า (aunt / older woman). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
