Timing the Joke in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for timing the joke in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with เออว่ะ (yeah), จริงว่ะ (that's damn true), ใช่ปะ (right?), ใช่ไหมล่ะ (right? / isn't it?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
