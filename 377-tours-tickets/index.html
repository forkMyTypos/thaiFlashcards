Tours & Tickets in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for tours & tickets in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with ทัวร์ (tour), ไกด์ (guide), ไกด์นำเที่ยว (tour guide), ตั๋ว (ticket). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
