เท่าไหร่

tâo-rài
falling-low tone
question word

how much

Remember it by

toe rye

A customer asking the price of a loaf of rye bread shaped like a giant toe.

In a sentence

  • อาหารจานนี้ราคาเท่าไหร่
    aa-hǎan jaan níi raa-khaa thâo-rài
    How much does this dish cost?
  • ทั้งหมดเท่าไหร่ครับ
    tháng-mòt thâo-rài khráp
    How much is the total?
