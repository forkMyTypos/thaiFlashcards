ใคร

khrai
mid tone
Pronoun

who?, someone, anyone

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

cry

A person crying while desperately looking around to find the unknown person they are searching for.

In a sentence

  • ใครอยู่ที่สถานี
    khrai yùu thîi sà-thǎa-nii
    Who is at the station?
  • ใครอยู่ที่ตลาด
    khrai yùu thîi dtà-làat
    Who is at the market?
