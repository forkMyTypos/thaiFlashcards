ทาง

thaang
mid tone
Noun; Preposition

way, path, road, direction, means, via, by way of

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

tang

In a sentence

  • ทางนี้ไปตลาด
    thaang níi bpai dtà-làat
    This way goes to the market
  • ทางนี้ไปสถานี
    thaang níi bpai sà-thǎa-nii
    This way goes to the station
  • เขาอยู่กลางทาง
    khǎo yùu glaang thaang
    He is halfway along the way.
  • ตลาดอยู่กลางทาง
    dtà-làat yùu glaang thaang
    The market is halfway along the way.
