ไม่

mâi
falling tone
Adverb

not

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

my

A person dramatically shakes their head and pushes away a giant forbidden object.

In a sentence

  • ฉันไม่กินพริก
    chǎn mâi gin phrík
    I don't eat chili.
  • ฉันไม่ชอบกาแฟหวาน
    chǎn mâi chɔ̂ɔp gaa-fɛɛ wǎan
    I don't like sweet coffee.
  • ฉันขอไม่ใส่พริก
    chǎn khɔ̌ɔ mâi sài phrík
    I'd like it without chili.
  • ฉันไม่ค่อยกินเนื้อวัว
    chǎn mâi khɔ̂i gin nʉ́ʉa wua
    I don't eat beef very often.
  • ฉันยังไม่อิ่ม
    chǎn yang mâi ìm
    I'm not full yet.
  • ฉันมีเงินสดไม่พอ
    chǎn mii ngoen sòt mâi phɔɔ
    I don't have enough cash.
