low tone
noun

In a sentence

    How many bottles are you buying?
  • ขายกี่ขวดครับ
    khǎai gìi khùat khráp
    How many bottles are you selling?
  • ใส่ขวดได้ครับ
    sài khùat dâai khráp
    You can put it in a bottle.\
