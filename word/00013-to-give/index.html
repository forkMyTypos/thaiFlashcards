ให้

hâi
falling tone
Auxiliary verb; Preposition; Verb

to give, for, let, allow, make [causative]

    hâi bai níi
    Give this leaf.

    hâi ngoen gàawn
    Give the money first.
  • ให้ตัวนี้
    hâi dtua níi
    Give this one.
