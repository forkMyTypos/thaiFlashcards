หมด

mòt
low tone
adjective, adverb

all gone, finished, to be completely out of, empty

Study it as flashcards

In a sentence

  • ฉันกินหมดแล้ว
    chǎn gin mòt láaeo
    I've finished eating it all.
  • ข้าวหมดแล้ว
    khâao mòt láaeo
    The rice is all gone.
