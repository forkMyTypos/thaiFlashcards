ชิ้น

chín
high tone
classifier

piece, classifier for pieces of food/cake

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

chin

A slice of cake balancing precariously on someone's chin.

In a sentence

  • ชิ้นนี้เท่าไหร่
    chín níi tâo-rài
    How much is this piece?
  • ชิ้นนี้ถูก
    chín níi thùuk
    This piece is cheap.
  • ขอชิ้นนี้อีกชิ้น
    khǎaw chín níi ìik chín
    Please give me another piece like this.
  • ชิ้นนี้มี
    chín níi mii
    This piece is available.
  • ขอชิ้นนี้ครับ
    khǎaw chín níi khráp
    This piece, please.
  • ขอชิ้นนี้
    khǎaw chín níi
    I'd like this piece.
  • ได้ชิ้นนี้
    dâai chín níi
    I've got this piece.
  • มีชิ้นนี้
    mii chín níi
    This piece is available.
