เรา

rao
mid tone
Pronoun

we, us, I [informal, can be used by both genders]

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

row

A group of Thai friends all rowing the same boat together.

In a sentence

  • เราสั่งอาหารแล้ว
    rao sàng aa-hǎan láaeo
    We've already ordered the food.
  • วันนี้เราทำอาหารที่บ้าน
    wan-níi rao tham aa-hǎan thîi bâan
    Today we are cooking at home.
