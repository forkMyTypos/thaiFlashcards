ใบ

bai
mid tone
Classifier; Noun; Verb

leaf, sheet, ใบขับขี่ driver's license;ใบเสร็จ receipt; [classifier for containers, sheets, tickets, fruit]; mute

Study it as flashcards

Remember it by

buy

A person buying a single green leaf at a store counter.

In a sentence

  • ขออีกใบ
    khǎaw ìik bai
    Please give me another one.
  • มีใบนี้
    mii bai níi
    This leaf is available.
  • ใบนี้เท่าไหร่
    bai níi tâo-rài
    How much is this leaf?
  • ใบนี้ถูก
    bai níi thùuk
    This leaf is cheap.
  • ขอใบนี้ค่ะ
    khǎaw bai níi khâ
    This one, please.
  • ขอใบนี้
    khǎaw bai níi
    I'd like this leaf.
  • ได้ใบนี้
    dâai bai níi
    I've got this leaf.
  • เอาใบนี้
    ao bai níi
    I'll take this leaf.
