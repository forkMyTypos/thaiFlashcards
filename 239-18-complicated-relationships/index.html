18. Complicated Relationships in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 18. complicated relationships in the Relationships & Dating Thai part of the course, starting with ความสัมพันธ์ซับซ้อน (complicated relationship), ไม่ชัดเจน (unclear / ambiguous), ยังไม่พร้อม (not ready yet), ไม่รู้ว่าเราเป็นอะไรกัน (don't know what we are). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.
