Thai Food – Chicken Dishes: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for thai food: chicken dishes in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ไก่ทอด (fried chicken), ไก่ทอดกระเทียม (garlic fried chicken), ไก่ผัดขิง (chicken stir-fried with ginger), ไก่ผัดเม็ดมะม่วง (chicken with cashew nuts). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
