Making Requests Naturally in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for making requests naturally in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ขอหน่อย (please give me), ช่วยหน่อย (please help), ช่วยดูหน่อย (please take a look), ช่วยบอกหน่อย (please tell me). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
