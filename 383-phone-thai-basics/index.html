Phone Thai Basics: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for phone thai basics in the Thai Phone Calls part of the course, starting with โทรศัพท์ (telephone), โทร (call; telephone), สาย (line), เบอร์ (number). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
