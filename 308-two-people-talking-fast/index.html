Two People Talking Fast in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for two people talking fast in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with คุยกัน (talk with each other), พูดแทรก (interrupt / speak over), เดี๋ยวก่อน (wait a moment), ฟังก่อน (listen first). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
