Hanging Out in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for hanging out in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with ไปเที่ยวกัน (let's go out / hang out), ไปไหนกัน (where are we going), ไปด้วยกัน (go together), เจอกัน (see each other). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
