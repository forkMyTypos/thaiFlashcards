Thai Food – Rice dishes: 14 words with audio

This lesson teaches 14 Thai words for thai food: rice dishes in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ข้าวมันไก่ (chicken rice), ข้าวหมูกรอบ (crispy pork rice), ข้าวหมูแดง (red pork with rice), ข้าวขาหมู (braised pork leg with rice). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
