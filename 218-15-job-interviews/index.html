15. Job Interviews in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 15. job interviews in the Professional Thai part of the course, starting with สมัครงาน (apply for a job), สัมภาษณ์งาน (job interview), ประสบการณ์ (experience), การศึกษา (education). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
