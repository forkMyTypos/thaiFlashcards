Fast, Messy Everyday Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for fast, messy everyday thai in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with เดี๋ยวก็รู้ (we'll know soon enough), เดี๋ยวค่อยดู (we'll see later), เอาไว้ก่อน (leave it for now), ช่างมัน (forget it / never mind). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
