The Thai alphabet

All 44 Thai consonants by class, 44 vowel forms and the tone marks — each with its name, its sound in romanization and an example word you can hear.

Each letter has a name — a word that starts with it, the way English says “A for apple”. Press ▶ to hear the example word. A computer voice saying a letter on its own is wrong too often to be worth hearing, so the letters themselves will be heard in a native speaker’s voice, once recorded. The tone each class gives is on the Tones page.
