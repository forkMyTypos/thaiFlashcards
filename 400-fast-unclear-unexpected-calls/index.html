Fast, Unclear & Unexpected Calls in Thai: 12 words with…

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for fast, unclear & unexpected calls in the Thai Phone Calls part of the course, starting with ใครโทรมา (who called?), ไม่ทันตั้งตัว (caught off guard), พูดเร็วไป (you're speaking too fast), ขอช้าลงหน่อย (please slow down a little). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
