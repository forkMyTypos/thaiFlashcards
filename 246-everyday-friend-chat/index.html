Everyday Friend Chat in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for everyday friend chat in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with เป็นไงบ้าง (how are you?), ช่วงนี้เป็นไง (how have you been lately?), ทำอะไรอยู่ (what are you doing?), กินข้าวยัง (have you eaten yet?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
