Explaining & Defending Your Position in Thai: 21 words…

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for explaining & defending your position in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ผมมองว่า (I see it as / I think that), ฉันมองว่า (I see it as / I think that), ผมเห็นว่า (I believe / I see that), ฉันเห็นว่า (I believe / I see that). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

I believe / I see that
