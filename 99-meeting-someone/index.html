Meeting Someone in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for meeting someone in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with นี่ใคร (who is this?), เขาชื่ออะไร (what's his/her name?), คุณชื่ออะไร (what's your name?), รู้จักกันไหม (do you know each other?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
