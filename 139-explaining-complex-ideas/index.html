Explaining Complex Ideas in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for explaining complex ideas in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with พูดให้ชัดเจน (explain clearly), อธิบายเพิ่มเติม (explain further), ขยายความ (elaborate / expand on that), หมายความว่า (mean). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
