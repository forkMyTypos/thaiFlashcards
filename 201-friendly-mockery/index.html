Friendly Mockery in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for friendly mockery in the Thai Humor & Banter -Warning. Mostly use with friends! part of the course, starting with ทำเป็นเก่ง (act like you're clever), ทำเป็นรู้ (act like you know), รู้ดีจังนะ (wow), ฉลาดจริงๆ (so clever). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

act like you're clever
ทำเป็นรู้ tham bpen rúu

you've got lots of admirers/contacts, huh
