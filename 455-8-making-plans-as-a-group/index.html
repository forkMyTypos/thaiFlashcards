8. Making Plans as a Group in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 8. making plans as a group in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with ไปไหนกันดี (where should we go?), ใครสะดวกวันไหน (what day works for everyone?), เอาที่ทุกคนสะดวก (choose what works for everyone), มีใครไม่กินอะไรไหม (does anyone have anything they don't eat?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
