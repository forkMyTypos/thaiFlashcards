10. Context Does the Work in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 10. context does the work in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with เอาอันนี้ (I'll take this one), เอาเหมือนเดิม (same as before), เหมือนเดิม (the same as before | unchanged), ที่เดิม (same place). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
