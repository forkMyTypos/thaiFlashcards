Comparing & Choosing in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for comparing & choosing in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with อันไหนดีกว่า (which one is better?), อันนี้ดีกว่า (this one is better), อันนั้นดีกว่า (that one is better), เหมือนกัน (same). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
