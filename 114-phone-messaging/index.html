Phone & Messaging in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for phone & messaging in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with โทรหา (call someone), โทรมา (call over here), โทรกลับ (call back), รับโทรศัพท์ (answer the phone). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
