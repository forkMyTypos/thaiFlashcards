Rare & Regional Thai Foods: 29 words with audio

This lesson teaches 29 Thai words for rare & regional thai foods in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with แกงฮังเล (northern pork curry), แกงโฮะ (mixed northern curry), แกงแค (northern vegetable curry), น้ำพริกหนุ่ม (green chili dip). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
