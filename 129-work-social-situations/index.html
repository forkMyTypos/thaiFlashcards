Work & Social Situations in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for work & social situations in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ประชุม (to meet), นัดประชุม (schedule a meeting), ประชุมเสร็จแล้ว (the meeting is finished), งานเสร็จแล้ว (the work is finished). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

Vocabulary (21 words)

to meet, assemble, meeting, assembly
นัดประชุม nát bprà-chum
schedule a meeting
ประชุมเสร็จแล้ว bprà-chum sèt láew
the meeting is finished
งานเสร็จแล้ว ngaan sèt láew
the work is finished
