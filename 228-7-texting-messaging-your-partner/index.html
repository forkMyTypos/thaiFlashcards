7. Texting & Messaging Your Partner in Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 7. texting & messaging your partner in the Relationships & Dating Thai part of the course, starting with ทัก (message / contact someone), ทักมา (message me / contact me), ตอบแชต (reply to a chat), หายไปไหน (where did you disappear to?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
