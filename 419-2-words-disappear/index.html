2. Words Disappear in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for 2. words disappear in the Fast / Messy Thai part of the course, starting with ไม่รู้ (don't know), ไม่รู้เหมือนกัน (I don't know either), รู้แล้ว (I know already), เอาไหม (do you want it?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
