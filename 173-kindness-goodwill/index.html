น้ำใจ: Kindness & Goodwill in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for น้ำใจ: kindness & goodwill in the Thai Culture & Social Nuance part of the course, starting with มีน้ำใจ (be kind / considerate), น้ำใจ (kindness / goodwill), ไม่มีน้ำใจ (be inconsiderate / unkind), ใจดี (kind). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
