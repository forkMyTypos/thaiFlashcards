Listening for Uncertainty in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for listening for uncertainty in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with น่าจะ (probably), คงจะ (probably), มั้ง (I guess / probably), มั้งนะ (maybe). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
