Natural Time Talk in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for natural time talk in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with เดี๋ยว (a moment), เดี๋ยวก่อน (wait a moment), เดี๋ยวนะ (wait a second), เดี๋ยวค่อยทำ (I'll do it later). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
