Characters & Cast in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for characters & cast in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with ตัวละคร (character; fictional character), นักแสดง (actor), พระเอก (male lead), นางเอก (female lead). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
