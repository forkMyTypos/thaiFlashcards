Health in Thai: 14 words with audio

This lesson teaches 14 Thai words for health in the Beginner part of the course, starting with เจ็บ (hurt), ปวด (hurt; ache; be in pain), ไข้ (fever), ไอ (cough; vapor; smoke). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
