4. Dates & Dating in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 4. dates & dating in the Relationships & Dating Thai part of the course, starting with เดต (date), ไปเดต (go on a date), แฟน (boyfriend / girlfriend / partner), คู่รัก (couple / romantic partners). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
