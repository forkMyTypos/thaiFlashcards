Ending Naturally in Thai: 15 words with audio

This lesson teaches 15 Thai words for ending naturally in the Natural Thai part of the course, starting with โอเค งั้นตามนี้ (okay), งั้นเอาตามนี้ (then let's go with this), เอาเป็นแบบนี้ (let's do it this way), เอาเป็นว่า... (let's say / let's settle on...). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.

Vocabulary (15 words)

okay, then let's go with this
งั้นเอาตามนี้ ngán ao dtaam níi

เอาเป็นแบบนี้ ao bpen bàep níi
let's do it this way
เอาเป็นว่า... ao bpen wâa...
let's say / let's settle on...
ไว้ค่อยคุยกัน wái khɔ̂i khui gan
let's talk about it later

we'll discuss it later
