Banks, ATMs & Money in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for banks, atms & money in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with ธนาคาร (bank), ตู้เอทีเอ็ม (ATM), กดเงิน (withdraw money), ถอนเงิน (withdraw money). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
