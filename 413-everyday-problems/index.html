Everyday Problems in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for everyday problems in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with ช้า (slow), สาย (line), รอ (to wait), รอนาน (long wait). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
