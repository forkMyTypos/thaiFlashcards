Suggestions & Advice in Thai: 21 words with audio

This lesson teaches 21 Thai words for suggestions & advice in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ลองไหม (want to try?), ลองดูไหม (want to give it a try?), ลองอันนี้ (try this one), น่าจะดี (should be good / probably good). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
