7. Changing the Subject in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 7. changing the subject in the Thai Two Person Conversations part of the course, starting with ว่าแต่... (by the way...), พูดถึงเรื่องนี้แล้ว (speaking of that...), อีกเรื่องหนึ่ง (another thing), ขอเปลี่ยนเรื่องหน่อย (let me change the subject). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
