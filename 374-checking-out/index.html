Checking Out in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for checking out in the Thai Tourism & Hotels part of the course, starting with เช็กเอาต์ (check out), คืนกุญแจ (return the key), คืนคีย์การ์ด (return the key card), เช็กบิล (check). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
