Services, Repairs & Appointments in Thai: 12 words with…

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for services, repairs & appointments in the Thai Phone Calls part of the course, starting with ช่าง (technician / repair person), ช่างซ่อม (repair technician), ซ่อม (repair), เสีย (to lose). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
