Emotional & Sensitive Conversations in Thai: 19 words with…

This lesson teaches 19 Thai words for emotional & sensitive conversations in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with พูดตรงๆ นะ (honestly / to be honest), ผมรู้สึกว่า (I feel that), ฉันรู้สึกว่า (I feel that), ผมรู้สึกไม่ค่อยดี (I don't feel very good about it). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
