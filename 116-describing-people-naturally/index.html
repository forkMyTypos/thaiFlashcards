Describing People Naturally in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for describing people naturally in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with หน้าตา (appearance; looks; face), หน้าตาดี (good-looking), ตัวสูง (tall), ตัวเตี้ย (short). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
