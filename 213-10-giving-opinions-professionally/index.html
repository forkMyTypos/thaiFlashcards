10. Giving Opinions Professionally in Thai: 10 words with…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 10. giving opinions professionally in the Professional Thai part of the course, starting with ความคิดเห็น (opinion), มองว่า (see it as / think that), คิดว่า (think that), เห็นว่า (consider that). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
