Making Friends in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for making friends in the Friends & Social Thai part of the course, starting with เพื่อน (friend), รู้จัก (know), รู้จักกัน (know each other), สนิท (close / be close). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
