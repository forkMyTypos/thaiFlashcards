Thai Food – Isaan food: 18 words with audio

This lesson teaches 18 Thai words for thai food: isaan food in the Thai Food part of the course, starting with ส้มตำไทย (Thai-style papaya salad), ส้มตำปู (papaya salad with crab), ส้มตำปลาร้า (papaya salad with fermented fish), ตำแตง (cucumber salad). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
