Opinions & Simple Reactions in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for opinions & simple reactions in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with คิดว่า (think that), คิดยังไง (what do you think?), คิดว่าไง (what do you think?), เห็นด้วย (agree; concur). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
