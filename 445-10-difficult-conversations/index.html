10. Difficult Conversations in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 10. difficult conversations in the Thai Two Person Conversations part of the course, starting with มีเรื่องอยากคุยด้วย (there's something I want to talk to you about), พูดตรงๆ ได้ไหม (can I speak frankly?), ผมไม่สบายใจกับเรื่องนี้ (I'm uncomfortable with this), ผมไม่ได้ตั้งใจทำให้คุณเสียใจ (I didn't mean to hurt you). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
