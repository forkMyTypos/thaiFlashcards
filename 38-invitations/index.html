Invitations in Thai: 14 words with audio

This lesson teaches 14 Thai words for invitations in the Beginner part of the course, starting with ชวน (invite), ไปด้วยกัน (go together), มาด้วยกัน (come together), ด้วยกัน (together). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
