Food Orders & Takeaway in Thai: 12 words with audio

This lesson teaches 12 Thai words for food orders & takeaway in the Real-World Situational Thai part of the course, starting with ใส่ (to put in), ไม่ใส่ (don't put in), เพิ่ม (to add), ลด (to reduce). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
