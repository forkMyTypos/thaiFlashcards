9. Group Dynamics and Social Signals in Thai: 10 words…

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for 9. group dynamics and social signals in the Thai Group Conversations part of the course, starting with ใครเป็นคนตัดสินใจ (who's making the decision?), ทุกคนรอเขาอยู่ (everyone's waiting for them), เขายังไม่ได้พูดเลย (they haven't spoken yet), ลองถามเขาดูสิ (try asking them). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
