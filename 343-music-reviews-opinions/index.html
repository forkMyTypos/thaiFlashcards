Music Reviews & Opinions in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for music reviews & opinions in the Thai Music & Lyrics part of the course, starting with ทำนอง (melody), จังหวะ (rhythm; beat; timing), เสียงร้อง (vocals), ดนตรีประกอบ (musical accompaniment). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
