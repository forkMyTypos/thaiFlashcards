Sarcasm, Humor & Teasing in Thai: 20 words with audio

This lesson teaches 20 Thai words for sarcasm, humor & teasing in the Thai Conversation part of the course, starting with ล้อเล่นนะ (I'm just kidding), ล้อเล่นหรือเปล่า (are you kidding?), พูดเล่นน่า (I'm joking), เอาจริงดิ (seriously?). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
