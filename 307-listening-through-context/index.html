Listening Through Context in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for listening through context in the Thai Listening part of the course, starting with บริบท (context), สถานการณ์ (situation), น้ำเสียง (tone of voice; voice quality), สีหน้า (facial expression; expression). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
