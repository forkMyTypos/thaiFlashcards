Live Shows & Events in Thai: 10 words with audio

This lesson teaches 10 Thai words for live shows & events in the Thai TV & Entertainment part of the course, starting with การแสดง (performance / show), คอนเสิร์ต (concert), เวที (stage), ขึ้นเวที (go on stage). Each word is shown in Thai script with tone colours, romanization and its English meaning, and you can hear it spoken at normal and slow speed.

Go through the cards at your own pace, then take the quiz to check what stuck.
